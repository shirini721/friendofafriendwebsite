The Concept

Every month, I make a reservation at a restaurant I've been wanting to try—the kind of place with a waitlist, a story, or a dish worth traveling for.

I invite three friends. Each of them brings one person I've never met: a colleague, a cousin, an old roommate, someone interesting.

By dessert, strangers become acquaintances. By the end of the night, acquaintances become friends. And next month, the circle expands again.

This is how communities are built—not through apps or algorithms, but through shared meals and trusted introductions.
